Atom Trap: Neon

Privacy policy · Last updated 27 October 2026

In short: Atom Trap: Neon collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Atom Trap: Neon is a game made by Christian Klinton. It's available as an Android app on Google Play and as a web game at todilo.github.io/atom-trap. This policy covers both.

What the game stores, and where

To keep your progress, the game saves these on your device only:

  • your settings (sound, music, theme, atom style, haptics, speed);
  • your high scores, including the name or initials you type next to a score;
  • puzzle results, Daily results and streaks, achievements, and game statistics such as walls placed.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes it.

What the game sends over the internet

The Android app sends nothing: the whole game is inside the app, and it plays offline. The web game is downloaded from GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address to deliver the files. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a Daily result, the game hands a short text (the Daily number, your result and a link to the game) to your device's share menu or clipboard. You choose where it goes. The game itself doesn't send it anywhere.

Permissions

The Android app asks for vibration, for the short buzzes on walls and captures (you can turn them off in Settings), and has the standard internet permission that apps built this way include. The game makes no use of the internet permission.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page, or open an issue at github.com/Todilo/atom-trap/issues.
