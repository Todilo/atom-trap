## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Atoms bounce around a glowing chamber. Draw a wall across it: the wall grows out from your finger in both directions, and once it reaches the sides, every space without an atom in it fills in. Fill three quarters of the board to clear it. But if an atom hits a wall while it's still growing, the wall breaks and you lose a life.

Simple to learn, hard to put down.

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY

• Classic: the original rules. More atoms every level, a clock that keeps ticking, and a high score to beat.
• Puzzle: 24 hand-made boards with pillars, rooms, spirals and corridors. Clear each one in as few walls as you can and earn up to three stars.
• Daily: a new board every day, the same for everyone. One scored try, then share your result and keep your streak going.
• Arcade: hunt the atoms. Seal each one into a tiny pocket to capture it, chain your catches for up to ×8 points, and race a single clock across the whole run.

MADE FOR PHONES

• Swipe to place a wall: the direction you swipe sets which way it grows.
• Short haptic buzzes when walls land and atoms are caught (you can turn them off).
• Plays fully offline.
• 22 achievements to unlock, five colour themes and twelve atom styles.
• Original synth soundtrack and sound effects, with separate volume controls.

NO ADS. NO PURCHASES. NO TRACKING.

Everything the game remembers stays on your phone.
